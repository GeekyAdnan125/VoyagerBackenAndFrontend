# 🌍 Voyager — Travel & Tourism Platform

> An integrated full-stack travel platform that brings destination discovery, trip planning, accommodation, transportation, local products, bookings, weather information, maps, community features, and travel assistance into a single application.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://voyager-01.vercel.app/

🔗 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/GeekyAdnan125/VoyagerBackenAndFrontend

---

## 📌 Overview

Voyager is a full-stack travel and tourism platform designed to simplify the travel planning experience.

Planning a trip usually requires travellers to switch between multiple platforms for discovering destinations, finding hotels, checking transportation options, understanding local attractions, checking weather conditions, finding local handicrafts, and managing bookings.

Voyager aims to bring these functionalities together in one platform.

A traveller can explore a destination and discover:

- 🏛️ Popular tourist attractions
- 🗺️ Nearby places and locations
- 🏨 Hotels and accommodation
- 🚌 Transportation options
- 🌦️ Weather information
- 🎨 Local arts, crafts and souvenirs
- 🤖 Travel-related assistance
- 👥 Travel community features
- 📍 Location and map-based features
- 🎫 Travel and hotel booking options
- 🛍️ Local products and online ordering

The platform also provides dedicated functionality for **hotel owners and local vendors**, allowing them to manage their listings, bookings, products, and orders.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Travel planning is often fragmented across different applications and websites.

A traveller may need one platform to discover tourist destinations, another to find hotels, another to check transportation, another to understand weather conditions, and another to purchase local products.

This creates a fragmented travel experience.

### Voyager addresses this problem by providing a unified platform for:

**Discover → Plan → Book → Explore → Shop**

It also connects travellers with local hotels and vendors, creating a small travel ecosystem instead of focusing only on destination discovery.

---

## 💡 Solution

Voyager provides a centralized platform where users can search and explore destinations and access travel-related information from a single application.

For example, if a user wants to visit **Goa**, Voyager can provide information related to:

- Famous places to visit
- Tourist attractions
- Nearby hotels
- Transportation options
- Estimated transportation costs
- Weather information
- Local arts and crafts
- Local souvenirs
- Travel-related recommendations
- Maps and location information

Users can also interact with hotels and local vendors through the platform.

# ✨ Key Features

## 👤 Traveller Features

- User registration and login
- Destination exploration
- Detailed destination information
- Tourist attraction discovery
- Nearby hotels
- Hotel booking
- Transportation information
- Weather information
- Weather comparison between destinations
- Maps and location-based features
- Travel-related chatbot
- Travel community
- Local souvenir discovery
- Online product ordering
- Travel safety and emergency-related utilities

---

## 🏨 Hotel Owner Features

Hotel owners have a dedicated dashboard where they can:

- Register/login as hotel owners
- Add hotels
- Manage hotel information
- Add and manage rooms
- Manage room-related information
- View bookings
- Manage hotel bookings
- View hotel-related analytics

The hotel owner functionality is separated from normal traveller functionality using role-based authorization.

---

## 🛍️ Local Vendor Features

Local vendors can use a separate vendor interface to:

- Create vendor accounts
- Add local products
- Associate products with a destination/locality
- Manage products
- View orders
- Manage customer orders
- Promote local handicrafts and souvenirs

This allows travellers to discover and purchase products associated with the places they visit.

---

## 🤖 Travel Assistant

Voyager includes a travel-focused conversational assistant for travel-related queries.

Users can ask questions such as:

> "What are the famous places to visit in Bihar?"

or:

> "Plan a trip from Kolkata to Jammu & Kashmir."

The assistant is designed around travel-related queries and helps users during the travel planning process.
